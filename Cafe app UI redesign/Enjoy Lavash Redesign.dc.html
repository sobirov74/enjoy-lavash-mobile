Six decisions that do the work
1A home screen exists. The app opened straight into Menyu, so a first-time user had to work out where they were. Home answers three things: what am I ordering (delivery or pickup, which branch), what do I have (ball balance), what did I order last.
2Order type is always on screen. One pill at the top of Home and Menyu: mode, branch, ETA. Tap it and a sheet changes both. Nobody reaches checkout unsure whether they picked yetkazib berish or olib ketish.
3Cart left the tab bar. An empty cart tab is dead weight. Instead a dark pill appears above the tabs the moment something is added, carrying the count and the running total.
4Two ways to add, both short. The + on a tile adds the standard version in one tap; the photo opens the options screen for people who want katta, sous and qo'shimchalar. Repeat orders skip the menu entirely.
5Ball is expressed in so'm. Your strings already say it — 5% ball, 1 ball = 1 so'm, buyurtmaning 50% gacha — so the design repeats that at every point money is mentioned: item CTA, cart, checkout toggle, success screen.
6Checkout is one numbered screen. Turi → manzil → to'lov → ballar, then the amount. No wizard, no hidden step, and the caveat about the final sum is stated instead of hidden.
Palette and type
Warm cream and a warm near-black, one red, gold for ball. Five colours total — the restraint is what reads as premium, not more hues. Manrope for anything expressive — greetings, prices, product names — over Golos Text for UI. Both carry Cyrillic, both are free for commercial use. Your Flutter build ships no custom font at all, so it currently renders in Roboto. Three pairings compared in 3a–3d. Dark values are your own AppThemeColors.dark — ground #151312, surface #1D1A18, borders #35302C. Gold drops to a 13% wash so it glows instead of glaring.
Assumptions
Uzbek (lotin), your default locale. Ru and En strings exist in the repo — say the word and I'll switch the mocks. Cashback only: no tiers, no stamps. 5% is a tweakable number. Payme, Click and ball. Naqd is not shown. Product names, prices and branches are stand-ins. Photos are real drop targets — drag an image onto any tile and it stays.

3 Shrift — three pairings that carry Cyrillic Clash Display has no Cyrillic, so it can't serve a Ru/Uz app. All three below cover Latin, Latin-ext and Cyrillic, are free for commercial use, and load in Flutter through google_fonts. The rest of the board is set in 3a.
3aManrope + Golos Text
My pick, and the current default. Manrope 700 does the expressive work; Golos Text was designed for Cyrillic interfaces and holds up at 11px. Closest in proportion to what the layouts were built on.
3bOnest, one family
Weight alone separates display from UI. Tighter and more contemporary; the trade is less contrast between a product name and the line under it.
3cLiterata + Golos Text
A serif on the greeting, product names and prices. Reads as restaurant rather than fast food — the most premium of the three, and the furthest from the current logo.
3dClash Display + Instrument Sans
The previous setting, kept for reference. Latin only — Uzbek lotin is fine, Russian is not.
